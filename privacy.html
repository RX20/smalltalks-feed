SmallTalks Privacy Policy

Effective June 15, 2026

SmallTalks gives you fresh conversation starters. We keep data collection minimal and you do not create an account.

What we collect

  • On your device only: your chosen interests, mode, audience description, and saved starters. These stay on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Push notifications (OneSignal): if you enable notifications, OneSignal processes a device push token and basic device identifiers to deliver daily alerts.
  • Subscriptions (RevenueCat / Apple): purchase and subscription status is processed to unlock premium features. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card details.
  • News content is fetched from a public feed we publish; browsing it does not identify you to us.

On-device AI

The optional "tailor" and "practice" features run on your device using Apple's on-device models. Your practice conversations are processed on-device and are not sent to our servers.

Third parties

OneSignal (push) and RevenueCat (subscriptions) process limited data as described above, under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your data.

Your choices

Disable notifications in iOS Settings; delete on-device data by removing the app; manage or cancel your subscription in the App Store. Contact us for any privacy request.
